Data Governance (NFR-02)

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • MUET Software Engineering DBMS Academic Project

1. Data Minimization & Academic Scope

This web platform operates strictly as an academic prototype for the Database Management Systems Complex Engineering Problem (DBMS CEP) at Mehran University of Engineering and Technology, Jamshoro. We collect only minimal essential information required to fulfill shopping workflow demonstrations: customer names, contact emails, delivery addresses, and order items.

2. Non-Storage of Payment Card Information

No real credit card numbers, debit card details, CVVs, or financial credentials are ever captured or stored on our servers or databases. All digital payments are routed through a simulated test gateway sandbox solely demonstrating ACID transactional workflows and payment attempt auditing.

3. Guest Session Tokens

Unregistered guest shoppers are issued a cryptographically signed cookie token storing a pseudo-anonymous session identifier. This token enables guest cart persistence across browsing sessions without mandatory registration and is automatically merged into account credentials upon voluntary registration or login.

4. Data Retention & Security Measures

User passwords are encrypted using salted PBKDF2 / SHA-256 password hashing. All database queries utilize parameterized SQL statements to eliminate injection risks.
