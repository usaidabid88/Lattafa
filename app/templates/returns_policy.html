Returns & Replacement Policy

Transparent guidelines for lodging return and refund requests

1. 7-Day Hassle-Free Returns

Customers may initiate a return or replacement request within seven (7) days of parcel delivery for merchandise that arrives damaged, defective, materially different from the catalog description, or incorrect.

2. Photographic Proof Requirement

To protect marketplace integrity and assist seller verification, claims involving damage or wrong items require uploading clear photographic proof (JPEG, PNG, or WebP; maximum 2 MB) showing the packaging and defect.

3. Seller SLA & Escalation Window

Sellers are required to review and respond to return claims within three (3) business days (72 hours). Claims remaining unanswered beyond this SLA threshold are automatically flagged on the administrative dashboard via database view v_overdue_return_requests for supervisory intervention.

4. Settlement Options

Approved returns may be settled via refund to the original payment channel, store credit voucher, or courier cash return upon pickup.
