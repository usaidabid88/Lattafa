Rules, disclaimers, and user obligations governing use of MUET Shop

1. Platform Operation & Educational Purpose

MUET Shop is a software engineering database laboratory application. Product listings, brands, and retail assets represent regional and national merchants used under academic fair-use guidelines to model real-world multi-vendor relational catalogs.

2. Order Placement & Pricing Parity

All order transactions placed through checkout are binding demonstrations governed by atomic transaction procedures (ACID). Item subtotals, provincial sales taxes, and delivery fees computed during checkout via stored procedures are verified for strict parity.

3. Seller Governance & Prohibited Actions

Sellers operating on the platform must undergo verification by administrative staff prior to catalog activation. Submitting fraudulent merchandise claims, malicious SQL payloads, or unverified returns is strictly prohibited and subject to automated audit logging.
